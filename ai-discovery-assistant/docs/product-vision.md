# AI Discovery Assistant

## Product Vision & Value Proposition

AI Discovery Assistant is a tool designed to support Business Analysts in preparing for client discovery meetings.

The tool helps analysts work faster and more efficiently by analyzing a business request together with the available documentation and identifying what is already clear, what is unclear, and what requires further investigation.


## Problem

Business Analysts may receive either a large amount of documentation or very limited information, such as a high-level business request or a list of topics.

In both situations, preparing for a discovery meeting can be time-consuming.

## Target User

Business Analyst or person who is performing the Discovery Analysis

## Goal

The final output is a concise summary of the analysis, highlighting:
- what is clear,
- what is unclear or missing,
- which topics should be further explored.


## Human-in-the-Loop

The tool supports the analyst's work rather than replacing their judgment or decision-making.

The analyst reviews, validates and decides which AI-generated insights and questions are relevant for the discovery meeting.

## Next phases

In a later version, the analyst will be able to add their own questions or suggestions and explore selected topics through an interactive conversation with AI.
