## # MVP Requirements

## V0.1 Features

1. **Business Request Input**
   - Provide a business request or topic.

2. **Document Upload**
   - Upload available documentation.

3. **Request & Document Analysis**
   - Analyze the request and provided materials.

4. **Initial Discovery Analysis**
   - Identify what is clear, unclear, missing, or requires further investigation.

5. **Discovery Questions**
   - Suggest questions for the client meeting.

6. **Concise Summary**
   - Summarize key findings and topics to explore.

7. **AI Validation**
   - Clearly indicate AI-generated content and require analyst validation.


## Out of Scope

- Interactive AI conversation
- Dynamic follow-up questions
- Analyst questions and suggestions during the analysis
- Iterative topic exploration
